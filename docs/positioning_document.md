# Positioning Synapse Data (TO BE REVIEWED)

## The problem Synapse Data wants to solve
Biomedical data has some of the most demanding data engineering requirements in the industry.
It exists in various formats including clinical notes, electronic health records, genetic sequencing data, protein structure data, regulatory networks, imaging, ...
Each one of these formats requires a deep understanding of the underlying clinical and biological reality to make sense of them.
Moreover, several of these data sources contain some of the most sensitive data imaginable about a person (name, health history, ...), correctly leading to increased regulatory and ultimately technical complexity.
This reality translates into organizational challenges:
- Bigger pharma companies consist of departments with deep and wildly different expertises (Discovery, Translational Science, Clinical trials, RWE, ...). 
    - This setup is good at developing the knowledge centers needed for this complex endeavour, but the challenge is to not let knowledge centers become knowledge silos.
    - This matters, because integrating data across these knowledge centers can unlock new opportunities to bring care to patients faster, better and cheaper.
- Smaller pharmaceutical companies might not have the funds to hire a team of specialists that are capable of filling all these roles.


## Vision
Synapse Data aims to promote a life science industry where biomedical data connects as fluently as synapses in the nervous systems.

## Mission
Synapse Data  builds the biological data mesh that pharma organisations need: 
- Compliant, scalable pipelines that bridge siloed biomedical sources and transform fragmented information into decisive insight for patients, researchers and company stakeholders.
- Data models that deeply encode clinical and biological domain knowledge in situations where scientific accuracy matters as much as engineering efficiency.

It executes on this mission by leveraging Databricks, a compliant cloud provider that is popular in pharmaceutical companies for complex, high volume workloads.
